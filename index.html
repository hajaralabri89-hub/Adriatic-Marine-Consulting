Onboard Advisory

We come onboard.
For the bunkering itself.

Pre-operation planning and risk review

We review the compatibility data, the bunkering plan, the SIMOPS, and the emergency shutdown procedures before anything starts. If something doesn't look right, we say so before the connection is made — not after.

Pre-bunkering meetings and crew briefings

We run the pre-bunkering meeting with your officers, the bunker supplier, and any other parties involved. We ensure everyone is aligned on the plan, communication protocols, and what to do if something changes.

Live operational presence during bunkering

We are on deck throughout the operation — monitoring flow rates, pressure, temperature, and connection integrity. We watch for early signs of issues and keep communication between the vessel, the supplier, and the port authority clear and structured.

Post-operation debrief and documentation

After completion we run a structured debrief with the crew, document any observations or non-conformities, and produce a written report with recommendations where relevant.

For many shipping companies, the first LNG bunkering operations are the most critical. Procedures may be new, the crew may not have done this before, and the consequences of a mistake — gas release, connection failure, or a poorly managed emergency — are serious.

Having an experienced person physically present on the vessel during the operation is a different level of assurance from having a procedure document. We have supervised more than 50 LNG bunkering operations in this capacity and know exactly what to look for, what questions to ask, and when to pause an operation.

This service is available for first bunkerings on a new route or with a new bunker supplier, for vessels with less experienced LNG-trained crews, and for operators who want independent oversight of their LNG bunkering programme.

We can also provide this support for methanol bunkering operations, applying the same structured approach to pre-operation review, live presence, and post-operation documentation.

Training

Training programmes

Delivered by Master Mariners with direct operational experience. Scenario-based, practical, and tailored to your fleet and crew level.

LNG Bunkering Training

Covers the full bunkering sequence — pre-operation compatibility checks, pre-bunkering meetings, connection and monitoring procedures, emergency shutdown, disconnection, and post-operation documentation. Delivered on site or in a simulator environment. Scenario-based with practical exercises and individual sign-off.

LNG Cargo Operations Training

Comprehensive training in LNG cargo handling — loading, discharging, boil-off management, cool-down and warm-up sequences, custody transfer, and emergency procedures. Aligned with IGF Code and SIGTTO guidance. Structured for officers moving to LNG vessels or refreshing existing knowledge.

Tanker Operations & Commercial Management

Practical training covering voyage operations, charterparty obligations, laytime calculation, demurrage principles, EU ETS reporting requirements, and vessel performance monitoring. Targeted at ship operators, commercial teams, and management staff who need to understand how these areas interact in practice.

Crew Competency Assessment

Structured assessment of officer competencies against operational and regulatory requirements — identifying gaps and producing individual development plans. Available as standalone assessments or integrated into broader SMS reviews. Delivered on site, on board, or remotely depending on the requirement.
