About

Senior advisory in LNG, gas carriers,
tankers, and decarbonisation.

Adriatic Marine Consulting provides senior advisory services across LNG and gas carrier operations, tanker commercial and fleet management, decarbonisation compliance, and maritime training. The firm is built on 40 years of combined sea-going and shore-based experience at the highest operational levels — covering LNG, VLEC (Very Large Ethane Carriers), LPG, crude, product, chemical, and methanol vessels.

Services are provided directly by the two principals, with no intermediaries. Clients receive the same level of engagement whether the assignment is a single bunkering oversight or a long-term fleet management advisory.

The firm works with shipping companies, charterers, energy companies, port operators, and maritime training centres across Europe, Asia, and the Middle East.

  • LNG, VLEC, and LPG cargo and bunkering operations
  • Tanker fleet management — crude, product, chemical, methanol
  • Commercial management under time charter
  • EU ETS, FuelEU Maritime, CII and EEXI compliance
  • ISM, ISPS and MLC internal audits
  • Crew training and competency assessment

The practice covers six core areas: LNG bunkering and cargo operations advisory, tanker commercial and operational management, decarbonisation and regulatory compliance, charterparty and voyage management, due diligence and technical reviews, and training programmes for deck officers and shore staff.

A dedicated onboard advisory service is available for LNG and methanol bunkering operations — providing experienced presence on the vessel throughout the full bunkering sequence, from pre-operation planning through to post-operation debrief and documentation.

Training programmes are practical and scenario-based, designed around actual operational procedures rather than standard course syllabi. They can be delivered on site, on board, or at simulator facilities, and are tailored to the specific vessel type and crew level.

Onboard Advisory

Onboard presence
during LNG bunkering operations.

Pre-operation planning and risk review

We review the compatibility data, the bunkering plan, the SIMOPS, and the emergency shutdown procedures before anything starts. If something doesn't look right, we say so before the connection is made — not after.

Pre-bunkering meetings and crew briefings

We run the pre-bunkering meeting with your officers, the bunker supplier, and any other parties involved. We ensure everyone is aligned on the plan, communication protocols, and what to do if something changes.

Live operational presence during bunkering

We are on deck throughout the operation — monitoring flow rates, pressure, temperature, and connection integrity. We watch for early signs of issues and keep communication between the vessel, the supplier, and the port authority clear and structured.

Post-operation debrief and documentation

After completion we run a structured debrief with the crew, document any observations or non-conformities, and produce a written report with recommendations where relevant.

This service provides an experienced senior consultant on the vessel for the duration of the LNG bunkering operation. It is intended for operators carrying out first bunkerings on a new route, using a new bunker supplier, or with a crew that has not previously handled LNG bunkering on that vessel type.

The scope covers the full sequence: pre-arrival review of compatibility data and the bunkering plan, attendance at the pre-bunkering meeting, monitoring of the operation from start to finish, and a structured debrief and written report on completion. Any concerns with the plan or the procedures are raised before the operation begins.

The consultant on site has supervised more than 50 LNG bunkering operations and is familiar with the range of vessel configurations, supplier arrangements, and port conditions that affect how an operation runs. The purpose is straightforward — to have someone present who has done this before and knows what to watch for.

The same service is available for methanol bunkering operations.

Training

Training programmes

Delivered by Master Mariners with direct operational experience. Scenario-based, practical, and tailored to your fleet and crew level.

LNG Bunkering Training

Covers the full bunkering sequence — pre-operation compatibility checks, pre-bunkering meetings, connection and monitoring procedures, emergency shutdown, disconnection, and post-operation documentation. Delivered on site or in a simulator environment. Scenario-based with practical exercises and individual sign-off.

LNG Cargo Operations Training

Comprehensive training in LNG cargo handling — loading, discharging, boil-off management, cool-down and warm-up sequences, custody transfer, and emergency procedures. Aligned with IGF Code and SIGTTO guidance. Structured for officers moving to LNG vessels or refreshing existing knowledge.

Tanker Operations & Commercial Management

Practical training covering voyage operations, charterparty obligations, laytime calculation, demurrage principles, EU ETS reporting requirements, and vessel performance monitoring. Targeted at ship operators, commercial teams, and management staff who need to understand how these areas interact in practice.

Crew Competency Assessment

Structured assessment of officer competencies against operational and regulatory requirements — identifying gaps and producing individual development plans. Available as standalone assessments or integrated into broader SMS reviews. Delivered on site, on board, or remotely depending on the requirement.
